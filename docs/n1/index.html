The Art Of Staying

Acrylic and ink on canvas · 80 × 100 cm · 2026

Available

Interested? Get in touch

I stand here, alone.

Here on this earth, surrounded by the people I love.
And yet, somehow, I still feel alone.

Alone with the thoughts wandering through my mind.
Thoughts that twist, turn and pull me under.
Dark thoughts.
Thoughts I cannot simply silence, escape or push away.

So I let them find their way onto this canvas.
Through colours, shapes and layers, I give them a place to exist.
I let the paint carry what my words sometimes cannot.

I can see the light.
I know it's there.
But right now, I cannot quite reach it.

Still, I keep looking.
I keep creating.
I keep staying.

And perhaps, one day,
I will find my way towards the light.

That is The Art Of Staying.
